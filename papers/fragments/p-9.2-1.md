Every boot enters fenced recovery stamped with a fresh durable nonce. The nonce file is updated atomically --- write, fsync, rename, parent-directory sync --- and the new value is used as the recovery input's tick, so per-node monotonicity holds across the two clock sources. Recovery needs a live quorum: a restarted node rejoins from quorum memory, installing the latest fenced view's history and re-applying its committed suffix. A rolling single-node restart therefore recovers from the surviving members, while a simultaneous full-cluster loss forfeits whatever the quorum held, and an operator re-bootstrapping a lost cluster must do so only after all outstanding leases can no longer be valid.
