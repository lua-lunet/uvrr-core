The replacement schedules of Sections~\ref{sec:results} and~\ref{sec:five-node-reincarnation} are stated longhand: one committed batch per era, the leader streaming the reincarnated standby between batches. This section states the machinery that executes such a schedule as one fused message round: the solver that computes and certifies the schedule before anything is sent, the wire encoding that packs it, and the repetitive construct that reduces its verification to the inspection of the head and the tail of the packed batch. The formalism is the present work's. The quorum geometry and the era-based agreement argument it preserves are Turner's~\cite{turner}; the underlying consensus invariants are those of Lamport~\cite{pms}.
