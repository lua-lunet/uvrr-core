Membership is fixed for one process lifetime, so the reincarnation eviction of Section~\ref{sec:method} --- the forced weight sequence that replaces the crashed identity with a new one --- is not exercised by this service. What the demonstration implements is the classic recovery boundary whose amnesia exposure motivates reincarnation, hardened with durable-nonce fencing: the fresh nonce is exactly the freshness ingress that the reincarnation design's stale-episode rule realises, and a restarting node can never complete recovery with a stale identity stamp. Converting the eviction sequence into this service is the next integration step.
