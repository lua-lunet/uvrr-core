Decision quorums follow the era of the \emph{slot}, not simply the era associated with a ballot. Consequently, continuing to decide later-era slots at $b$ also requires a quorum valid for those slots. A value accepted under a leader that has failed is committed at the same slot by the new leader's per-slot install; it is not a new value at the slot and not a violation. In the concrete pivot below, the active quorum is a majority in both adjoining eras.
