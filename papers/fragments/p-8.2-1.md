An application of strong consistency is the replication of small state by consensus: the configuration authority Lamport, Malkhi and Zhou describe~\cite{reconfig-sm}, and Vertical Paxos's auxiliary configuration master~\cite{vertical-paxos}, are the precedents. This design embeds such an authority in the application --- for small metadata, with ordinary replication performed in memory.
