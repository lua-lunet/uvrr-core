The design kills one voting node that is not the leader, rotated across all non-leader voting nodes of the three-datacentre \code{lunet-locks} cluster, for $k=\mathrm{XXX}$ kill/rejoin cycles, with continuous competing lock traffic at the lease cadence throughout. Each iteration begins only after the previous rejoin has completed serving and a soak interval has elapsed, so no measurement is contaminated by the previous one. For each iteration the harness waits for steady state, kills the node's process --- Crash-Stop: volatile state lost, the node is by construction a different node on reopen --- and the iteration ends when the reincarnated node has rejoined as a weight-0 standby, been walked back to voting weight by the leader's forced sequence, and is again a voting, serving replica. The metric is the kill-to-rejoin-serving latency, one sample per iteration: p50 X.XX~ms, p90 X.XX~ms, p99 XX.X~ms, max XX.X~ms, mean X.XX~ms, and the count of eras and view changes consumed per rejoin, X.
