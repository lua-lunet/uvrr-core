The replacement schedules of Sections~\ref{sec:results} and~\ref{sec:five-node-reincarnation} return a failed member to voting weight through committed batches. They presume the replacement can reach a fence: it must know an era the cluster has committed before its engine sees a message. A node whose durable state is lost returns blank; a node that reincarnates under a fresh identity holds no roster entry at all. Neither can guess an era, and the one-era window drops what it cannot span. The rejoin path closes this with a gossip layer outside the voting protocol; the addendum~\cite{artifact} gives its full construction, its worked scenarios and the kernel-checked safety conditions. Three properties carry the design, none of which touches a quorum rule.
