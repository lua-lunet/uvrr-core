This design takes a different trade. A client reaches the service through an ordinary load balancer at \emph{any} member. At the leader the exchange is one round trip; at a follower the contacted node forwards the operation to the leader and answers the commit itself, so the client observes one round trip plus a one-way forwarding leg. No leader-aware routing is required for correctness; a load balancer that learns the leader's identity trades a fraction of a round trip for the availability of random assignment.
