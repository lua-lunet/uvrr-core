A node outside the cluster --- a lost durable state or a fresh identity --- rejoins through the gossip path of Section~\ref{sec:rejoin-gossip}: the join gossip to every known node, the witness list acted on by the leader, and the streamed keep-up to the first fence. The wire carries the gossip as its own message type, and the explicit-message tests pin the whole exchange; the section's safety argument is the contract those tests hold the implementation to.
