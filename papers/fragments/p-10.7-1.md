Every placeholder above carries an implicit order of magnitude, and the campaign checks the consistency before any number is quoted. The rules the lab book enforces: no loopback-harness figure may be quoted beside a cloud claim (loopback round trips are tens of microseconds; intra-zone cloud round trips are not); every failover figure must be at least the suspicion floor plus two measured round trips of the same rig --- a failover quoted an order of magnitude faster than that rig's RTT is a measurement error, not a result; every flush figure must be at least the device class's write latency --- a flush quoted an order of magnitude below the block device's measured \code{fsync} is the same class of error; and ratios may only be formed within one rig and one run. Table~\ref{tab:consistency} lists the checks; the lab-book template carries one row per figure and no figure may enter the paper with its row blank.
