The claim under test is diskless low latency: rejoin through reincarnation is faster than paying a forced disk flush at the reincarnation boundary. The decision rule for the campaign: if the measured rejoin latency of E1 is slower than the forced-flush baseline of E2 at the same sample count, the diskless claim collapses --- if rejoining is slower than doing disk flushes, nothing has been achieved. The system under test for all four designs is the \code{lunet-locks} demonstration of Section~\ref{sec:demo}. Every number below is a placeholder of the form X.XX, showing the significant figures and decimal places the run will report --- latencies in milliseconds with two decimals for the local rig and one decimal at tens of milliseconds, counts as integers --- to be filled at run time from the lab book, one figure per lab-book row.
