Oki and Liskov introduced Viewstamped Replication in 1988~\cite{vr}. Liskov and Cowling's 2012 Viewstamped Replication Revisited (VRR) eliminated disk writes during normal processing and view changes~\cite{vrr}. Replication therefore retained the state needed for agreement without adding a local disk flush to each update. The remaining difficulty was how to bring a replica back after loss of that state.
