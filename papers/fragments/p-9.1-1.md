The adapter instantiates the concrete core replica over a segmented log with weighted-majority quorums, running volatile stability: protocol state lives in quorum memory, an empty journal is provisioned at boot, and the only bytes the service ever fsyncs locally are the recovery nonce file. The adapter retains the tick clock (a monotonic value clamped against wall-clock regression), exactly-once reply correlation keyed by the client's message identifier, and guard checks that re-validate every replicated operation entry --- in Prepare, DoViewChange, StartView, RecoveryResponse, and NewState messages --- against the lock service before the entry reaches the core. The Teal/Lunet wrapper owns sockets, TCP framing, peer source validation, and forwarding; a UDP peer envelope carrying a membership fingerprint guards the fixed membership at the transport boundary, so differently configured clusters cannot merge. An append-only lock-event journal, a REST/WebSocket feed, and a console are observability only: the journal never participates in replication or recovery, and a journal error disables journaling without affecting the service path.
