Read against the construct, verifying a fused reconfiguration reduces to its ends: the head admits or refuses the envelope whole, and the tail fixes the exit assertion, while the body~\eqref{eq:loop-body} is the per-slot fold. The mechanised ladder carries the three pieces of this reading, each with its stated scope~\cite{artifact}. The accept direction is finite response-quorum telescoping: a fixed response set eligible at one era is eligible at the next under the preservation hypothesis, and with every responder acknowledging all the packed slots, every slot holds quorum-backed evidence; the concrete eligibility of every row of both replacement schedules --- the three rows of the three-voter schedule and the seven rows of the five-voter one --- is mechanised beside it. The first transition telescopes the rest: for the three-node two-transition schedule, a base quorum at the first era and a preservation step across the first boundary give quorum eligibility at every slot through the last era, and the equal-total swap $(1,2,1,2) \to (2,1,2,1)$ is the negative control --- the response set $\{B,D\}$ is a majority at the first era and a minority at the second, so the preservation hypothesis is load-bearing rather than decorative. That theorem assumes the quorum family is preserved across the boundaries; it does not perform phase one, and it is not a refinement of the executable system. The commit direction runs from the tail: the accept path admits an entry only as the next slot, so per-node acceptance is downward closed, and under a fixed configuration --- the stable-leader setting of no view change and no era change inside the range, one quorum family, the preservation step the identity --- a quorum's acceptance of the range's last slot is already its acceptance of every slot in the range, so a commit naming the last slot decides them all; the next-slot guard is load-bearing, since a responder that accepted slot 1 while skipping slot 0 defeats the argument even with the last slot unanimous. Phase-one authority for every packed slot is the header ballot itself: the distinguished leader executes phase one once for all future instances at one proposal number until interrupted~\cite{pms}. The telescoping records that induction at the envelope's granularity --- one header ballot that is both phase one and phase two for every packed command, the batch processed as a slab whose only interruption point is the envelope boundary --- and the quorum telescoping alone performs no phase one.
