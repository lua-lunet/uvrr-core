The packing and the construct together remove the per-message round trips. The solver computes the whole schedule before anything is emitted; the leader packs it as one envelope per recipient; one request--reply round collects the fused acknowledgements; one batch-commit broadcast follows, and the reincarnated node learns of its membership on the push of the telescoped reconfiguration. The three-voter replacement carries no scaling operation, so both of its boundaries cross on their riders inside the same commits: the whole reincarnation completes in one round trip. The five-voter schedule packs the same way, its two scaling boundaries crossed by the leader-preserving view change between the envelopes of its unit-mass batches. End to end the rejoin is the classic two round trips, the theoretical Paxos optimum~\cite{pms}: the gossip answer of Section~\ref{sec:rejoin-gossip} and the reconfiguration round. No forced flush stands anywhere on the path --- the boot fence is written only at a clean shutdown and asynchronously at startup, so neither reconfiguration nor reincarnation pays a disk flush (Section~\ref{sec:method}) --- the deferred-flush economics that make the round affordable at all~\cite{netdisk}. A crashed node therefore reads only a trivial amount of state at startup: the demonstration process boots in double-digit milliseconds including the fence reads, the node rejoins in two round trips, and the cluster returns to full capacity in dozens of milliseconds on commodity hardware.
