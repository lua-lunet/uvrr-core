The client protocol has three operations, one JSON object per line over TCP. Every request carries a message identifier, a stable client identifier, an increasing request number, and a lock identifier; retries reuse the exact envelope, and replication deduplicates by (client, request number), replaying the prior reply. \code{get} returns the live lease on a lock, or null. \code{set} grants or renews a lease --- a lease identifier, a holder identifier, and an expiry --- and a lease is live only when its expiry is later than the execution time; an expired candidate is rejected, an absent or expired incumbent is free, and a live incumbent may be renewed or replaced only by the same holder. \code{release} removes a live lease only when both holder and lease identifier match, and is idempotent for a missing or expired lease. Lease expiry is what makes the diskless design safe to operate: a crashed holder's locks free themselves when the lease lapses, without any recovery path trusting the holder's lost state.
