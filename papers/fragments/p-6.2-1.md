Every node that hears a join request records the sender on an operator visible witness list; the leader alone acts on the list. It replays the contiguous suffix of its journal from the advertised frontier, in order, each entry through the ordinary accept path --- there is no witness-specific accept path --- and then streams every subsequent phase-2 and commit to the witness. The witness never votes: quorum membership is decided by the configuration alone. Because the list rides at every node, a successor leader resumes the stream on election without waiting for a re-announcement, so failover does not interrupt keep-up. When a committed batch makes the witness a member, every node removes the entry; delivery as both voter and witness in the window between the commit and the removal is covered by deduplication and merely wastes IO.
