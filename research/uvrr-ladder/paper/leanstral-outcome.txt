Result of the Leanstral drafting experiment (ladder/08-leanstral-lemma2.md): FAILED within a 17-minute timebox. The agent produced a 105-line draft with a sound proof plan — weighted sums distribute over list append, a sublist's weighted sum is bounded by the total, two disjoint sublists of a duplicate-free list together sum to at most the total, and the single node on which the scaled weights differ gives the contradiction — but the file does not compile. It uses the Mathlib keyword lemma and the Mathlib names add_le_add_left and List.sum_append_nat, none of which exist in Lean core, despite the prompt and file header forbidding Mathlib. One of its own Lean checks became a runaway process holding roughly 30 GB of memory and the session had to be stopped.

Assessment: Leanstral through the vibe lean agent is useful as a planner and a fast route to a first draft, and it is the only Leanstral channel that has produced compiling code in this project; but for Mathlib-free targets it still needs a human (or a stricter harness that rejects Mathlib identifiers before running Lean) to land a proof. The general Lemma 2 therefore remains open; the concrete schedule of Rung 7 stands on its own.
